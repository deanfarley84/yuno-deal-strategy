The field — and the structural split that decides this deal

Two kinds of "orchestration" exist: acquirers routing volume to themselves, and neutral layers with nothing to earn from the routing decision. The distinction matters more than any feature list.
The wedge, used calmly and only once: "Whoever routes your transactions — ask what they earn when the answer is 'to ourselves'." Adyen is credible, which is exactly why the conflict-of-interest point lands harder when made without cynicism. Never disparage them; refuse their battlefield instead.

The conflict that decides the deal — and how we refuse it

Sofia is measured on revenue recovery. David demands 20% cost reduction. Most sellers pick a side.
The winning move is to refuse the trade-off: the $18M decline leakage is itself the largest cost line in HealthBridge's payment stack — bigger than the whole $14.8M processing bill. Framed that way, Sofia's agenda becomes the fastest route to David's number, and the two mandates stop competing. This reframe is the spine of the whole proposal.
